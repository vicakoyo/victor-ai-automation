# 05 — IT Support Triage Workflow

**Platform:** Microsoft Forms, Power Automate, Microsoft Teams  
**Status:** Workplace implementation / portfolio documentation in progress

## Overview

A simple internal service workflow created to give employees a structured way to report technology issues.

Microsoft Forms captured the request, Power Automate processed it and Microsoft Teams was used to notify the appropriate people for triage and ownership.

## Business Problem

Technology issues were often raised informally, making it harder to capture problems consistently, establish ownership and follow up.

The workflow introduced a structured route for issue reporting, triage and assignment.

## Workflow

```text
Employee
    ↓
Microsoft Form
    ↓
Power Automate
    ↓
Teams Notification
    ↓
Triage
    ↓
Owner / Support Provider
