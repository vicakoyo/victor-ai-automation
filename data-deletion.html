Last updated: August 15, 2026

How to Request Deletion

Users may request deletion of personal information associated with their interactions with Victor AI Automation.

To request deletion, send an email to: victor.akoyo@outlook.com

Please include:

  • The WhatsApp phone number used to interact with the service.
  • A clear statement that you are requesting deletion of your data.

What Will Be Deleted

Where applicable, information associated with your use of the service, including message-related information and identifiers retained by the application, will be deleted unless retention is required for legitimate legal, security, or compliance purposes.

Processing Your Request

Additional information may be requested where reasonably necessary to identify relevant records and verify the request.

Privacy Policy
